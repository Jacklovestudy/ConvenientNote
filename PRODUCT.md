# Product

## Register

product

## Users
This is a personal desktop note and todo canvas used by the app owner for everyday task capture, arrangement, and quick follow-up.

## Product Purpose
Convenient Note provides a lightweight sticky-note workspace where todos can be created, edited, checked off, and spatially arranged on a canvas. Success means notes feel fast to capture, easy to scan, and satisfying to move around.

## Brand Personality
Sticky, lively, personal. The interface should feel like a digital sticky-note board with enough polish and motion to make arranging notes enjoyable.

## Anti-references
Avoid dry enterprise dashboards, heavy form-driven task managers, quiet monochrome productivity tools, and static interactions that make the canvas feel inert.

## Design Principles
- Keep the note board direct: creation, editing, and movement should stay immediately reachable.
- Preserve the sticky-note metaphor while making the desktop interaction feel polished.
- Use motion to make drag, snap, and feedback states feel responsive and energetic.
- Favor low-friction personal workflows over complex team-management structure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
No formal WCAG target is specified yet. Keep text readable, maintain keyboard-safe core actions where practical, and avoid motion that blocks task completion.
